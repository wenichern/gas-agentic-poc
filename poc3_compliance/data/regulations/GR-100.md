# GR-100 Gas Distribution Leak Survey Rule (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE, replace with real regulation text)

## 4.1 Definitions
A Class 3 location is an area with more than 46 buildings intended for human occupancy, or a place of public assembly.

## 4.2 Survey interval
Operators must survey Class 3 locations at intervals not exceeding 12 months.

## 4.3 Repair grading
Grade 1 leaks require immediate action. Grade 2 leaks must be repaired within 6 months. Grade 3 leaks are monitored.
