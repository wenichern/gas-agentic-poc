# RB-2026-14 Regulatory Bulletin: Distribution Leak Survey Amendments (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE)
Effective date: 2026-12-01
Compliance report due: 2027-01-15

## R1 Leak survey interval
Operators must survey Class 3 locations at intervals not exceeding 6 months (previously 12 months).

## R2 Records retention
Leak survey and repair records must be retained for 5 years (previously 3 years).

## R3 Odorant verification
Odorant concentration must be verified monthly at 3 sample points (unchanged).
